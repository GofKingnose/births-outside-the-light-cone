## What the paper is about

A quantum register grows: fresh qudits are born in a pure state, uncorrelated with the rest, and the
register is scrambled by local dynamics. Does the entropy of a region catch up with its maximum when
scrambling is fast? A Frautschi-type velocity criterion says yes; simulations refute it. The paper
shows that the controlling variable is causal:

- **Theorem A.** For every unitary circuit, on any graph and with arbitrary births,
  S(A) ≤ log q · |Ā ∩ Inf(A)|, where Inf(A) collects the qudits whose world line meets the causal past
  of A. **Theorem B** is a Lieb–Robinson version for local Hamiltonians.
- **Theorem C.** For an isotropic front R ∝ t^α, the deficit is extensive for every local dynamics if
  α ≥ 1, and can be made sub-extensive if α < 1. In one dimension the causal bound is attained
  (Proposition A1).
- **Theorems D and E.** For gates drawn from a 2-design, the average purity of a prefix reduces exactly
  to the walk of a single domain wall absorbed by the line of births. This gives E[G] = Θ(m^{2−1/α}) for
  1/2 < α < 1 and, for one birth every k layers, the exact annealed Rényi-2 density θ_k.
- **Numerics.** Stabilizer simulations up to 6400 qubits and Haar state-vector simulations up to 22
  qubits, with predictions written before the computations; failed predictions are reported.
- **Energy cost.** In a free lattice field with a vacuum, uncorrelated births cost an energy of the order
  of the cutoff, so the cosmological reading remains a constrained analogy.

## Reproducing

Requirements: Python 3.13 with the packages of `requirements.txt`; optionally Coq 8.18 and pdflatex.

```sh
pip install -r requirements.txt
./reproduce.sh quick    # tests, Coq, exact computations, spot checks, analyses, figures (~10 min)
./reproduce.sh full     # + all Monte Carlo campaigns except the Haar campaign (~30 min more)
./reproduce.sh haar     # Haar state-vector campaign of Table 4 (several hours)
./reproduce.sh paper    # compiles paper/main.pdf
```

All seeds are fixed in the scripts, so the campaigns rewrite `data/` and `field_test/` with the same
content. `code/spot_checks.py` re-runs single jobs of the long campaigns and compares them with the
stored records in a few seconds. Scripts are run from their own folder (`cd code; python3 script.py`).

Coq: `coq/check_coq.sh` compiles the five proof files and prints, through `coq/Assumptions.v`, the axioms
used by the main results (none for the combinatorial statements; only those of the standard `Reals`
library for the real-number lemmas).

## Status and limitations

The paper states, for each result, whether it is proven, what is checked in Coq and what rests on cited
results (Table 10). The main limitations are listed in Section 10.5: Theorems D and E assume a 2-design
and bound the annealed Rényi-2 entropy; the typical case in two dimensions is a conjecture; Haar
simulations are limited to 22 qubits; the model has no energy, and in a theory with a vacuum the
birth condition is excluded by its energy cost.
